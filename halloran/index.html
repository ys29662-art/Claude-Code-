The House of Halloran

A workshop that has
never moved street.

In 1887, Edmund Halloran hung his sign on St John's Lane, in the heart of Clerkenwell — the quarter where London's finest movements have been finished for three centuries. We are still there.

Every Halloran is the work of one hand from first jewel to final regulation. We make a few hundred watches a year, not a few hundred thousand. That is the point. A Halloran is not bought to be replaced; it is wound each morning and passed on.

— Arthur Halloran, Fifth Generation
